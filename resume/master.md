## 12. Voice & Style Rules (when tailoring)

Lifted from CLAUDE.md / portfolio brand to keep voice consistent across resume, LinkedIn, and portfolio:

- **Proof over promise.** Every claim has a link or metric. "Architected platforms" is banned; "Scaled an ERP to 5,000 active subs at Fooddarzee" wins.
- **Calibrated honesty.** Never claim "live & paying" if not paying. "Live in production" or "shipping to first customers in [date]" is fine. Overclaiming kills credibility at senior level.
- **Terse.** No "passionate about", no "helping companies", no "synergies", no "scalable solutions."
- **Active voice. Past tense for done work, present for current.**
- **Metrics-led bullets.** Lead with the number when one exists.
- **Confident, not arrogant.** Opinionated where earned. No hype.
- **One accent per bullet.** Don't bold three things in one line.
- **No emojis. No icons in bullets.** Keep it ATS-clean.

## 13. Tailoring Workflow (reference)

1. Drop JD into `ats-resume-tailor` (or use the `resume-tailor` skill in Claude Code).
2. Tool extracts: required skills, preferred skills, role archetype, seniority, keywords.
3. From this master doc, select:
   - 1 headline (§2)
   - 1 summary (§3)
   - 3–5 bullets per role (§4)
   - 1–2 project deep-dives (§5) — almost always lead with Fooddarzee + Sivon HQ
   - Reordered skills matrix matching JD priority (§7)
4. Output: `resume/tailored/YYYY-MM-company-role.md`
5. Review → tighten → render to PDF → `resume/exports/`.
6. Commit. The tailored file is now a permanent record of what you sent where.

**Target time per tailored resume: 30–45 min.** If longer, master doc needs another pass to fill gaps.
